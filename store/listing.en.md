## Description (App Store 4,000 chars, Google Play 4,000 chars)
Build a mining colony on the edge of the galaxy. Tap a crystal, buy your first drone and
watch a handful of crystals turn into an empire that keeps mining while your phone is
in your pocket.

MINE WHILE YOU ARE AWAY
Devices work on their own. Come back in an hour or in the morning and collect what the
colony mined meanwhile. Longer offline mining and boosts are entirely your choice.

13 DEVICES, 61 UPGRADES
From a mining drone through refineries and orbital stations to a black hole and a
multiverse mine. Every device has four upgrade tiers and your tap grows with the colony.

PRESTIGE AND STARDUST
When a run slows down, prestige. You earn stardust that permanently speeds up mining,
or spend it on star upgrades: a quick start, cheaper devices, the Golden Vein with a
chance of a hundredfold tap. A thousand stardust founds a new galaxy and everything
multiplies again.

28 ACHIEVEMENTS, DAILY REWARDS
Every achievement gives a permanent +1 % production. A seven-day reward streak greets
you whenever you return.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
No forced ads, no waiting for energy. A video boost or a stardust pack only when you
want it.

• Works offline, progress saves automatically
• Czech and English
• Vibration, animations and sounds can be turned off

## Content rating
No violence, no gambling, no social features. In test mode purchases charge nothing;
after billing is connected declare "In-app purchases", after ads "Contains ads".

## Privacy notes (Data safety / App Privacy)
The game currently collects no data and sends nothing to a server. Saves stay on the
device. After adding accounts, billing or ads the questionnaire must be updated (device
identifiers for ads, e-mail for accounts, purchase history).
